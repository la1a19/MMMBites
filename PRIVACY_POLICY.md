# MMMBites Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 10, 2026

MMMBites ("we", "us", or "the app") is a meal-memory journal that lets you save photos, locations, moods, and notes about meals you eat and share them with friends you choose. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the controls you have over your data.

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect what is necessary to run the app's features.

**Account information**
- Email address (used to sign in and recover your account)
- Username (a display name you choose)
- Profile photo (optional — you choose whether to upload one)

**Content you create**
- Photos you attach to memories or albums
- Album titles, tags, descriptions, and cover photos
- Memory details: title, mood, best bite, notes, "what made it memorable" tags
- Place names and coordinates **you type or select** for a memory or album (we do not access your device's GPS location automatically)
- Friend connections you make in-app (via QR scan or username search) and reactions left on shared memories

**Device permissions (only when you trigger them)**
- **Camera** — to take a photo for a memory or to scan a friend's QR code
- **Photo library** — to choose photos for memories, album covers, or your profile

You may decline either permission. The relevant feature will simply be unavailable until you allow it.

## 2. What We Do **Not** Collect

- We do not access your precise device location (no GPS tracking).
- We do not collect contacts, calendars, microphone, or health data.
- We do not run any analytics SDK that tracks you across other apps or websites.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used **only** to:

- Authenticate you and keep you signed in.
- Save and display your albums, memories, and profile.
- Show shared memories to the friends you explicitly tag or share with.
- Generate in-app summaries ("highlights", "recap") from your own content.
- Maintain app reliability (basic crash diagnostics provided by Apple's standard system reports).

## 4. Third-Party Services We Use

MMMBites runs on Google Firebase. The following Firebase products process your data on our behalf:

- **Firebase Authentication** — manages your email/password login.
- **Cloud Firestore** — stores your profile, albums, memories, friend connections, and reactions.
- **Firebase Storage** — stores the photo files you attach to memories and your profile picture.

Firebase / Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

These services act as data processors. We have configured them to store your data in regions selected by Google's default routing.

## 5. Sharing With Other Users

You decide what is shared:

- A memory or album is private until you tag a friend or set it as shared.
- Tagged friends can see the memory and react.
- Removing a tag immediately revokes that friend's access.

We never expose your email, raw photos, or notes to anyone you did not explicitly tag.

## 6. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

## 7. Your Rights

You can:

- **View and edit** any memory, album, profile field, or friend connection at any time inside the app.
- **Delete a single memory or album** by opening it and tapping the delete option.
- **Delete your entire account.** In **Settings → Account → Delete account**, the app will permanently remove your profile, all your albums and memories, all friend requests you sent or received, and your sign-in credentials. This action cannot be undone.

If you have a legal request (data export, correction, or any rights granted by your local law such as GDPR / CCPA / PIPA), contact us at the address below.

## 8. Children

MMMBites is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can delete it.

## 9. Security

- All traffic between the app and Firebase uses HTTPS / TLS.
- Passwords are hashed and managed by Firebase Authentication — we never see them in plain text.
- We restrict access to production data to the project maintainers.

No system is perfectly secure. We work to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and notify you in-app or by email. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
